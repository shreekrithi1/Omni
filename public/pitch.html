Problem statement

Life admin is scattered.
You're still the glue.

Every household runs on a dozen apps, inboxes and portals. None of them talk to each other — so a person has to.

Bills slip through

Statements land in different inboxes and portals. Overcharges and due dates go unnoticed.

Schedules collide

School, work and appointments live in separate calendars. Conflicts surface too late.

The mental load piles up

Forms, follow-ups and reminders sit in your head instead of getting done.

Tools inform, not act

Dashboards and assistants tell you what's wrong. You still do the clicking.

The missing piece is someone to do the work between your apps.Not another dashboard. Not another reminder.
03 / 07
Solution

Agents do the work.
You make the call.

Omni is a personal operating room: a team of specialist AI agents, led by Olivia, that watches your inboxes and accounts, prepares the next step, and waits for your yes.

1 · Notice

Bills, school mail and calendar changes arrive in agent inboxes.

2 · Prepare

Specialist agents research, cross-check budgets and draft the action.

3 · You approve

One inbox of decisions. Payments and messages never go out without you.

4 · Done

Omni executes, logs every step, and tells you what changed.

MoneyNet worth, bill audits, anomalies
FamilyShared calendar, forms, logistics
WellbeingSleep, HRV, daily vitality
OliviaChat or face-to-face video briefings
